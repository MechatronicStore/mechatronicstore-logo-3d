The code in this repository is MIT licensed. The brand is not. The name

`assets/`, are trademarks of MechatronicStore (Chile).

These terms are deliberately generous. Print the parts, show them off, teach
with them. The only thing we keep a hand on is selling and passing yourself off
as us.

## Yes, go ahead

No permission needed, no need to tell us:

* Print parts carrying the logo for yourself, your workshop, your office, your
  classroom or your makerspace.
* Give those printed parts to anyone: friends, students, customers, coworkers.
* Post photos and videos of them anywhere, including monetized channels. Tag us
  if you feel like it.
* Use them in a workshop, class, tutorial or course, including one you charge
  for. You are charging for your teaching, not selling our brand.
* Charge for material and machine time when you print a part for someone else,
  the way a print shop does.
* Modify the code in any way, including changing the size, depth, placement or
  finish of the logo.

## Ask first

One email, and the usual answer is yes:

* **Selling parts that carry the logo.** Marketplaces, your own shop, fairs,
  wholesale, anything where the branded part is the product being sold.
* Using the logo in your own product line, packaging, app icon or brand.
* Anything that suggests we made it, endorsed it, sponsored it or partnered on
  it.

Write to **ventas@mechatronicstore.cl** with what you want to make and roughly
how many. A short written OK is all it takes, and it is normally granted.

## No

* Presenting yourself, your shop or your project as MechatronicStore, or as an
  official reseller, partner or sponsee.
* Redrawing, recoloring or stretching the artwork and still calling it the
  MechatronicStore logo. A modified mark is not the mark.
* Using the logo on anything illegal, hateful, or that a reasonable person would
  read as ours when it is not.

## Why selling needs a yes

Not to make money on licensing. It is that a badly printed part with our logo on
it, or a buyer who believes they bought an official product and comes to us for
warranty, lands on our name and not on yours. A one line email keeps both sides
clear. These terms may get looser over time; they will not get stricter for
anyone who already asked and got a yes.

## Using this tool with your own logo

Nothing in the code is MechatronicStore specific. Point `--logo` at any SVG and
the pipeline behaves exactly the same:

```bash
python logo3d.py --model part.stl --logo my-own-logo.svg
```

Flat, filled paths in a handful of solid colors give the best results. Your logo
stays yours: this repository claims nothing over it.
